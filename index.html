01
Petrol Pump Construction

GasBuddy constructs complete petrol retail outlets — from land levelling and civil foundations to underground fuel storage tanks, canopy structures, dispensing equipment, electrical installations, signage, and full site handover. We work with all major oil marketing companies in India including IOCL, BPCL, and HPCL, delivering outlets that meet the latest standards.

Fuel Retail

HT/LT supply, transformer, ATM kiosk, CCTV, and automation systems.
Finishing & Landscaping

02
Highway Amenities

We design and build complete highway wayside amenity complexes for NHAI and private operators — including food courts, clean restrooms, petrol pumps, CNG/EV stations, rest areas, truck parking, and retail kiosks. Our amenity complexes serve millions of highway travellers across India's national highway network.

CNG Stations

GasBuddy is among India's leading CNG station builders — having delivered 500+ CNG outlets across 28 states. We handle full EPC including cascade systems, compressors, buffer storage, dispensing units, civil works, safety systems, and PNGRB approvals. Our CNG stations are engineered for maximum uptime and safety.

CNG · Clean Fuel

PNGRB Compliance

Who We Are
About
GasBuddy

Founded in 2009 with a single petrol pump project in Delhi. Today, GasBuddy is one of India's most respected multi-sector infrastructure companies — 500+ projects, 28 states, 15+ years of delivery excellence.

Our
Story.

GasBuddy was founded in New Delhi in 2009 by a team of engineers who believed India needed an infrastructure contractor that could go deeper, deliver better, and think longer-term than the fragmented players in the market.

We started with a single petrol pump contract in Gurugram. Within three years, we had delivered 100 CNG stations. By 2015 we were empanelled with Indian Railways. Today we operate across 9 sectors, 28 states, and have become a trusted name for some of India's largest organisations — IOCL, BPCL, NHAI, and Indian Railways among them.

Our growth has been built not on aggressive sales, but on repeat work from clients who trust us to deliver. That trust is GasBuddy's most valuable asset.

Mission & Vision

Our Mission: To deliver world-class infrastructure that powers India's growth — on time, on budget, and built to last for generations.

Our Vision: To be India's most trusted integrated infrastructure company, leading the transition to sustainable, green energy infrastructure while continuing to build the physical foundations of a stronger nation.

Our Promise: Every project we take on, we deliver as if it were our own. No shortcuts, no excuses, no compromises on safety or quality.
