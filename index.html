Our Story.

GasBuddy was founded in 2009 by a team of engineers who believed India needed an infrastructure contractor that could go deeper, deliver better, and think longer-term.

We started with a single petrol pump in Gurugram. Within three years, 100 CNG stations. By 2015 we were empanelled with Indian Railways. Today we operate across 9 sectors, 28 states, with 800+ team members and ₹2000 Cr+ in delivered project value.

Our growth has been built on one thing: repeat business from clients who trust us. That trust is GasBuddy's most valuable asset and the foundation of everything we do.

Our Vision: To be India's most trusted integrated infrastructure company, leading the transition to sustainable, green energy infrastructure while continuing to build the physical foundations of a stronger nation.

Our Promise: Every project we take on, we deliver as if it were our own. No shortcuts. No excuses. No compromise on safety or quality. Ever.

What types of infrastructure does GasBuddy build?
▼
GasBuddy operates across 9 infrastructure sectors: petrol pump construction, highway amenities, CNG stations, EV charging stations, gas storage and transportation equipment, national highway works (roads, bridges, flyovers), Indian Railways works (civil, track, safety, energy), metal fabrication and metallizing, and pre-engineered buildings (PEB). We are truly a one-stop infrastructure partner.
Is GasBuddy an approved Indian Railways contractor?
▼
Yes. GasBuddy has been fully empanelled as an approved Indian Railways contractor since 2015. We are approved for civil works, bridge works, track works, electrical and energy works, and specialised works including rail fracture detection services, hydrogen train components, and solar power plant installation across all Railway zones.
Do you handle EPC (turnkey) projects or only civil works?
▼
GasBuddy handles full EPC (Engineering, Procurement, and Construction) turnkey projects across all our sectors. This means we manage everything from initial design and engineering, through procurement, to construction, commissioning, and handover. We also undertake standalone civil works contracts where clients have their own engineering or equipment supply.
Can GasBuddy supply gas storage equipment without construction?
▼
Absolutely. Our gas equipment division operates independently of our construction division.
