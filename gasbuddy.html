Our
Services

A complete portfolio of infrastructure services — from fuel stations and gas storage to Indian Railways works, highways, metal fabrication, and green energy systems.

01
Fuel & Energy Retail

GasBuddy builds complete fuel retail infrastructure — petrol pumps, CNG stations, and the new generation of EV charging stations. We handle everything from civil works to commissioning.

Fuel · CNG · EV
Petrol Pump Construction
Full civil, electrical, underground tank, dispensers, canopy, and site finishing.
Highway Amenities
Rest areas, food courts, washrooms, and traveller facility complexes.
CNG Stations
Cascade systems, compressors, dispensers, civil, and full commissioning.
EV Charging Stations
AC/DC fast chargers, smart management, canopy, and electrical works.
02
Gas Storage & Transportation

We design, fabricate, and supply industrial gas storage and transportation equipment — from CNG cascades to specialised helium and nitrogen containers for industrial and scientific use.

Gas · Storage · Transport
CNG Cascades
High-pressure CNG cascade systems for storage and fast-fill dispensing.
Nitrogen Quads
Tube trailer quads for industrial nitrogen transportation.
Helium Quads
Specialised helium containers for scientific and industrial transport.
Type-1 & Type-4 Cylinders
Steel seamless and composite lightweight cylinders for all gas types.
03
National Highway Works

GasBuddy executes major national highway projects for NHAI and state agencies — road construction, bridges, and flyovers using the latest materials and construction technologies.

Roads · Bridges · Flyovers
Road Construction
Sub-base, DBM, bituminous concrete, drainage, and road markings.
Bridges
PSC girder, steel composite, RCC bridges — fabrication, launching, erection.
Flyovers
Grade separators and flyover structures for traffic decongestion.
Retaining Walls & Drainage
All ancillary highway civil works and site development.
07
Indian Railways

Our flagship sector. GasBuddy delivers the full spectrum of Indian Railways works — structural, safety, energy, and cutting-edge green technology including hydrogen train systems.

Railways · Green · Safety
Heritage Station Beautification
Art, murals, and beautification works at railway heritage stations.
FOB / ROB / RUB / LHS
Foot over bridges, road over/under bridges, and level height structures.
PSC Slabs & Ballastless Tracks
Steel girder replacement with PSC slabs; modern slab-track systems.
Rubberised Level Crossings
Safety pad installations at manned and unmanned level crossings.
Rail Fracture Detection
Ultrasonic automated fracture detection services for track safety.
Solar & Hydrogen Systems
Solar power plants, solar items, hydrogen train components & storage.
Rail Tunnel Works
Tunnel boring, lining, waterproofing in challenging terrain.
Locomotive Electrical Parts
Electrical components, assemblies, and spares for railway locomotives.
08
Metal Fabrication & PEB

GasBuddy's fabrication division handles structural steel bridges, metallizing for corrosion protection, steel warehouses, and pre-engineered buildings for industrial and railway clients.

Metal · Fabrication · PEB
Metallizing — Indian Railways
Thermal spray metallizing for corrosion protection of railway bridges and structures.
Steel & Metal Bridges
Fabrication and erection of structural steel railway bridges.
Steel Warehouses
Design-build of conventional and pre-engineered steel warehouses.
PEB Structures
Pre-engineered factories, sheds, and commercial buildings.

About
GasBuddy

Over 15 years of building India's infrastructure — with integrity, innovation, and an unwavering commitment to excellence.

Our
Story.

Founded in 2009, GasBuddy started as a fuel station construction company in Delhi. Today, we are one of India's most trusted multi-sector infrastructure firms — delivering projects from Kashmir to Kanyakumari across 9 sectors and 40+ work types.

Our growth has been driven by one principle: build what India needs, the right way.

Our Mission & Vision

Mission: To deliver world-class infrastructure that powers India's growth — on time, on budget, and built to last.

Vision: To be India's most trusted integrated infrastructure company, leading the transition to sustainable, green energy infrastructure.
